# The town and wild zones (post-MVP)

Both are separate areas reached from the nursery. Not in the MVP.

## The town
A small hub of 4–6 clients, each with a questline told through their orders. It holds the story, rare rewards
and rumours that hint at hidden crosses. Deliberately light: no full Stardew village. Over time players
uncover what clients really want the plants for.

## Wild zones (to design)
- A few biomes, each with its own wild species: a bog, an old forest, a ruined monastery garden.
- One trip per day; in co-op someone stays behind to mind the nursery.
- Carrying capacity depends on cart upgrades.
- No combat: feral plants misbehave, and players handle them with their own tool-plants.
- Source of breeders' notes and hints for hidden crosses.
- Still open: how far, how dangerous, how often.
