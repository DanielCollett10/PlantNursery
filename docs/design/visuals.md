# Visuals

## Greybox placeholder colours (M1 onwards)
Use flat URP Lit materials, one per function, generated by an Editor script:

| Function | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Benches, shelving, floor furniture | Brown | `#8B5A2B` |
| Plug trays, pots, plants (base) | Green | `#4CAF50` |
| Water equipment (can, tap, hose) | Blue | `#2F7FD8` |
| Vehicles, trolley | Yellow | `#F2C230` |
| Customers | Purple | `#9C5BD1` |
| Vendors | Orange | `#E8833A` |
| Ground / plot | Muted grass | `#7A9A5A` |
| Building (computer, bed, delivery shelf) | Light grey | `#BDBDBD` |
| Ghost valid / invalid | Translucent green / red | `#40FF4080` / `#FF404080` |

- Plants: a simple stem (green) with leaf shapes tinted by the `leafColour` phenotype (green `#3FA34D`, red `#C0392B`, yellow `#E5C02A`).
  They are scaled by stage (Seed 0.1 → Mature 1.0). Wilting plants droop and desaturate, and dead plants turn brown-grey `#6E6253`.
- Every placeable is a prefab: behaviour and footprint on the root, placeholder mesh as a swappable child.

## Lighting
URP with a basic day–night cycle driven by the sim clock: warm morning, neutral day, orange evening, blue night.

## Final art (later)
Stylised low poly in the spirit of My Time at Sandrock.
